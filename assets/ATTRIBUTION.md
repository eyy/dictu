# where the pictures come from

Both images in this directory are details of **one manuscript page**: the incipit of
John of Garland's *Dictionarius*, the book that gave the word "dictionary" its name.
He wrote it in Paris around 1200 for his students — a list of the trades they saw in
the street, in Latin with Old French glosses between the lines. This copy was made
about a century later and survived only because a bookbinder used it as waste.

The line reads:

> **Dictionarius** dicitur iste libellus a dictionibus magis necessarias, quas tenet
> quilibet scolaris …

*"This little book is called a dictionarius, from the more necessary words that every
scholar keeps …"*

## how to fetch them again

The source is IIIF, so any crop can be re-cut without asking anyone:

```
manifest  https://iiif.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/iiif/manifest/4021d35f-e1df-409f-a5b9-96dfa8cd417b.json
image     https://iiif.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/iiif/image/3b4ed413-f9bd-460f-a857-2d6e5f592d22
icon      …/3b4ed413-f9bd-460f-a857-2d6e5f592d22/1940,1330,175,175/512,/0/default.jpg
incipit   …/3b4ed413-f9bd-460f-a857-2d6e5f592d22/1900,1325,540,180/1400,/0/default.jpg
```

`region` is `x,y,w,h` in the full image's own pixels (2940×1975).
